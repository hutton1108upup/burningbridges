ABOUT THE TREND

What is Gas Station Dance AI?

Gas Station Dance AI is a photo-based version of the gas station dance meme. The idea is to put yourself or your friends into its dance, selfie and car-exit scenes.

BEFORE YOU START

Questions about photos and videos

How do I get started with Gas Station Dance AI?

Watch the dance videos, choose solo or trio casting and select clear photos for each scene. The View credit plans button opens Pricing. Review the included templates and output settings before choosing a plan.

Can I use one photo for all three shots?

Yes. Choose Just me and select one clear full-body photo. It represents the same person in all three scenes.

What photos can I select?

Use JPG, PNG or WebP files up to 8MB each and at least 512 × 512 pixels. Each photo should show one person clearly.

Are my photos uploaded or saved?

No. Your selected photos stay in this browser tab. Reloading or closing it clears them.

Is this free, and do I need an account?

Watching videos and previewing photos is free and needs no account. Visit Pricing to compare paid plans, included templates and credit costs.

Is music included?

The videos have sound and start muted. Use the player controls to unmute. For your own edit, choose music you have permission to use.

What does the video preview show?

It shows the dance, selfie and car exit in order. The two examples in the video section use different people.
