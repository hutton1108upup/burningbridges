What is the Bulin 47 AI trend?

The Bulin 47 AI trend puts a different performer into the street freestyle. You can recognise the clip by the microphone, the speaker in front and the crowd packed behind the performer.

Compare the original clip with the AI versions on this page. Pay attention to the face during movement and the way the camera follows the performer.

Photo tips

Choose a clear photo.

A sharp photo makes facial details easier to see. Avoid heavy filters and tight crops.

What works well

  • Keep both eyes and the hairline visible.
  • Use soft, even light and a sharp image.
  • Include the shoulders and upper body.
  • Choose one person, with their permission.
Read the photo guide →

What to check in a video

  • Watch facial detail during fast movement.
  • Look for changes in hands and the background.
  • Check audio and mouth movement separately.
  • Confirm the chosen tool supports this exact trend.

What is a Bulin 47 AI video maker?

No. You can watch the clips and check a photo here. Our paid generator makes the Burning Bridges party video; buying credits does not give you a Bulin 47 template.

What kind of photo should I choose?

Use a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB. Pick one well-lit person with their face and shoulders visible. Use your own photo or get permission from the person pictured.

Does choosing a photo upload it anywhere?

No. The photo stays on your device. You can preview it here and remove it with the Remove photo button.

Do the examples include sound?

Yes. The clips start muted; use the player controls to turn on sound. The audio is part of these references.
