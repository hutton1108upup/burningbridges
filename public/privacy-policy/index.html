Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for Burning Bridges AI Video - AI Video Generator

Introduction

This policy describes the current preview website at burningbridgesaivideo.pro. Account registration, payments and AI video generation are not connected in this version.

Information We Collect

Accounts and photo previews

We do not collect account details or payment information through this preview. A photo you choose is displayed locally in your browser using a temporary object URL. It is not uploaded to our servers or used to train AI models. Closing or reloading the page clears the preview.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to understand page visits, traffic sources, devices and engagement. Google receives technical browser and device information and uses analytics cookies to distinguish visits. Our code removes query strings and fragments from the page and referrer URLs sent to GA4. We do not send photo contents, filenames, email addresses, prompts or payment details as analytics events. Google signals, advertising personalization and automatic enhanced measurement are disabled.

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand clicks, scrolling and page interaction through heatmaps and session recordings. Microsoft receives browser/device information, interaction data and page URLs and may use cookies to recognize visits. Strict text masking is enabled. The photo preview and generator area are explicitly masked before Clarity loads, as are input fields. Clarity is not loaded on URLs containing a query string. Analytics scripts run only on the production domain, not on local or Workers preview hosts.

Cloudflare hosts this website and processes network information, such as IP addresses and HTTP requests, to deliver pages and protect the service. Google Search Console provides search performance and indexing reports; it does not add a tracking script to this website.

How We Use Information

We use aggregate analytics and masked interaction reports to identify navigation issues, improve page performance and understand how visitors find and use the website. No actual purchase or AI generation is recorded as completed by this preview.

Data Storage and Security

Selected photos remain in your browser. Website analytics are processed by Google and Microsoft under their own retention and security policies, potentially in countries other than yours. Requests to this website and our analytics providers use HTTPS.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Our current service providers are Cloudflare for hosting, Google for visit and search analytics, and Microsoft for masked behavior analytics. We may also disclose information if required by law. Payment processors and AI generation providers are not connected in the current version.

Read the Google Privacy Policy, Microsoft Privacy Statement and Cloudflare Privacy Policy for their processing practices.

Your Rights

You may contact us about personal data we control or privacy questions. There is currently no account database or stored photo to access or delete on this preview website.

Cookies

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity may set analytics cookies. These are used for measurement and behavior analysis, not for a login session. You can block or delete cookies and block third-party scripts in your browser. This website does not currently provide an on-site consent or preference manager.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for children under 13. Please contact us if you believe we have received personal information from a child.

Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy when the site's functionality or data processing changes, including before connecting accounts, payments or AI generation.
