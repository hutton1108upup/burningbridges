What happens in the story

Four scenes carry the pet from the cabin into a memory.

01 / THE CABIN

A familiar face in the dark

In a dim cabin, the person faces a pet that has changed. The animal edges closer across the floor.

02 / THE CLOSE-UP

The pet has turned

Clouded eyes and rough fur give the pet its zombie look. The close-up holds long enough for the change to register.

03 / THE LEAP

You open your arms

The person lowers the gun. The pet runs forward and jumps, turning a tense moment into a familiar greeting.

04 / THE MEMORY

Back in the golden field

The same jump continues in a field at sunset. The healthy pet lands in its owner’s arms.

Pet photos that work best

  • One animal looking toward the camera.
  • Enough light to show the fur and markings.
  • The full head, including both ears.
  • No costume or toy covering the face.
  • A simple background around the pet.

Use a photo of your own pet and a portrait you have permission to use.

Zombie Pet video FAQ

Photos, story details and plans.

What is a Zombie Pet AI Video?

A short fictional story about a person and their pet. It starts in a dark cabin with a zombie animal, then cuts to a warm memory of the healthy pet jumping into its owner’s arms.

Can the story feature a cat?

Yes. Both dogs and cats fit the story. Choose a photo with the whole head visible so the ears, coat pattern and face are easy to see.

What kind of pet photo should I choose?

Use one animal in daylight, facing the camera. Keep the ears inside the frame and remove anything covering the face. A simple background makes the pet easier to distinguish.

What should the person’s photo look like?

Choose a clear, front-facing portrait with even light. Keep the face unobstructed and use a photo of yourself or someone who agreed to appear.

Is the pet a zombie throughout the story?

The cabin scenes use the zombie look. The final scene shows the healthy pet in a sunlit field, which gives the story its change in mood.

Can I add two pets?

Use one pet for this two-character scene. A group photo makes it harder to tell which animal should take the second role.

How much does an AI video plan cost?

Are the photos uploaded when I add them here?

No. The two photo previews stay on this page in your browser. Opening Pricing does not upload them or start a video job.
