Start with a Reference Performance

The photo form uses a prepared template rather than a free-form prompt field. The reference shows the intended setting and action, leaving you to choose a portrait and the settings for your idea.

What the Reference Communicates

  • The party room, lighting and surrounding people.
  • The lead performance, gestures and camera movement.
  • The soundtrack and the timing of the hook.
  • The relationship between the portrait and performer.

These are creative directions, not guarantees that every detail will remain unchanged.

Keep Appearance and Action Separate

A portrait communicates face, hair and clothing. A video reference communicates action and setting. Small patterns, hands, accessories and overlapping faces deserve attention when reviewing a result.

Choose a Photo, Format and Resolution

Use one image under 8MB. Compare Auto, 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 formats and 480P, 720P Standard and 1080P Pro credit estimates. A template gives a focused starting point; it does not guarantee identical outcomes across portraits.
