Burning Bridges Troubleshooting: Photo, Task and Result

Resolve photo-preview problems, task and billing confusion, and picture or sound defects without mistaking reference playback for a new render.

Updated October 13, 2026

  • Photo and account issues on this site

First identify the kind of problem

Is the issue in photo selection, an account or payment, task submission, playback, or a completed video result? Keep the task or order reference for support, but never send passwords or recovery codes.

If a returned clip does not feature your portrait, check the task and result label first. A reference sample is not your generated video; review the submitted result for identity, framing, motion and sound, then follow the account support steps if it is wrong.

Photo and account issues on this site

Symptom What to do
Photo does not preview Choose an image under 8MB. Export a fresh JPEG or PNG and try again. If a preview cannot be shown, do not submit.
Draft is missing Return in the same browser. Removal, sign-out and age cleanup can clear the local draft. Select the photo again.
Not enough credits The current party video uses 100 credits at a fixed 720P, 16:9 setting. Check your balance and pack validity before purchasing.
Payment returned but balance did not change Use Check payment status if shown. The return URL alone does not issue credits; verified payment confirmation is required. Do not buy again while checking.
Video could not be submitted Keep the error details and any task reference. Check the task status and balance before trying again. If a task is pending, running or needs review, do not create another submission while it is being checked.
Result will not play or download Check task state, sign-in and browser connection. A protected account download requires its owning account. Retain the job reference if it continues.

Picture and sound problems

Problem Useful next check
Face changes or looks wrong Use one sharp, front-facing portrait with less occlusion; reduce conflicting changes.
Wrong lead or duplicated face Use one clear subject in the photo. For a result from this site, retain the task reference and contact support if the lead is wrong or duplicated. The fixed photo form has no prompt editor.
Hands or cup distort Inspect the affected moment and retain the task reference. The current template has fixed movement and length; a higher resolution or another paid attempt does not guarantee a correction.
Output looks soft Check source sharpness and actual output dimensions. Upsizing cannot recover missing detail.
Content request rejected Read that service’s reason and policy; use permitted media and its appeal path for a mistaken rejection. Do not try to evade its restrictions.
Audio missing or post muted Check whether audio was included and which sounds are permitted for your publishing account. See the audio guide.

Failure, quality and refunds are different

A confirmed failed charged task without delivery returns its generation credits under our refund policy. A completed result that you dislike is not automatically the same as a system failure. Cash refunds are reviewed under the policy; third-party tools have their own rules.

Email support@burningbridgesaivideo.pro with the issue, time, browser and order or task reference. This page lists the support address; it does not promise a response time.
