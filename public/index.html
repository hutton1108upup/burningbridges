What to Check in an AI Video Result

Watch the whole result before using it. Check picture, identity and sound together; examples show creative directions rather than a measured success rate.

Face and identity

Watch the whole clip, especially close-ups and overlaps. Check whether the person stays recognizable as the camera moves.

Hands and occlusion

Pause where a hand, cup or another person passes across the face or body. Look for distorted shapes or sudden appearance changes.

Mouth and sound

Listen with sound on. Check whether audio is present and whether mouth movement matches it.

Clothing and framing

Check visible outfits, fine details and the edges of the frame. A higher resolution alone does not prove a better likeness or more natural motion.
