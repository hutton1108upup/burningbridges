Photo quality

Choose: a sharp, well-lit photo with one unobstructed face. Avoid: blur, sunglasses, heavy filters and several people in the same image.

What to upload

If an outfit matters to your idea, include the upper body and visible clothing rather than a face-only crop. Small logos, jewelry and fabric patterns may be reinterpreted in AI edits; outfit matching has not been tested on this site.

Resolution

The form offers 480P, 720P and 1080P sample settings. Resolution describes image detail; it does not establish a closer likeness or more natural movement. No output is generated in this preview.

What to Check in an AI Video Result

This is a review checklist for a future generated clip. Our generation service is not connected or tested yet; the existing examples do not establish a success rate.

Face and identity

Watch the whole clip, especially close-ups and overlaps. Check whether the person stays recognizable as the camera moves.

Hands and occlusion

Pause where a hand, cup or another person passes across the face or body. Look for distorted shapes or sudden appearance changes.

Mouth and sound

Listen with sound on. Check whether audio is present and whether mouth movement matches it.

Clothing and framing

Check visible outfits, fine details and the edges of the frame. A higher resolution alone does not prove a better likeness or more natural motion.
