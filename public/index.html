Choose a Useful Reference Photo

Prepare a photo that clearly shows the appearance you have in mind. You can inspect it locally here without starting a generation or paying.

Photo quality

Choose: a sharp, well-lit photo with one unobstructed face. Avoid: blur, sunglasses, heavy filters and several people in the same image.

What to upload

If an outfit matters to your idea, include the upper body and visible clothing rather than a face-only crop. Small logos, jewelry and fabric patterns can change in an AI edit. Check these details throughout a result.

Resolution

The current template uses 100 credits for a 720P, 16:9 video. Resolution does not guarantee a closer likeness or more natural movement.

What to Check in an AI Video Result

Watch the whole result before using it. Check picture, identity and sound together; examples show creative directions rather than a measured success rate.

Face and identity

Watch the whole clip, especially close-ups and overlaps. Check whether the person stays recognizable as the camera moves.

Hands and occlusion

Pause where a hand, cup or another person passes across the face or body. Look for distorted shapes or sudden appearance changes.

Mouth and sound

Listen with sound on. Check whether audio is present and whether mouth movement matches it.

Clothing and framing

Check visible outfits, fine details and the edges of the frame. A higher resolution alone does not prove a better likeness or more natural motion.
