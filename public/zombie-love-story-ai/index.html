THE STORY IN THREE SHOTS

The leap ends in a memory.

The Zombie Love Story AI format connects two versions of the same people. The cabin feels cold and tense. The field shows a moment before the infection. The cut keeps the leap moving into the hug.

01 / THE CHOICE

Lower the gun

The survivor recognizes their partner beneath the infection. Instead of firing, they lower the weapon and reach out.

02 / THE LEAP

Carry the movement

The infected partner runs forward and jumps. Leave room around both people so the leap reads clearly before the cut.

03 / THE MEMORY

Finish with a hug

The light turns warm. The same partner is healthy again, held in an embrace. The field is a memory, not a cure in the cabin.

Original storyboard illustrations. AI video can change faces, hands and the timing of a cut; review those details in any finished clip.

TWO FACES TO RECOGNIZE

Choose the roles.
Then choose the photos.

A partner, a friend or a family member can play either role. What matters is recognizing the same face in the cabin and the field.

  • Use one adult per photo, with their whole face in frame.
  • Choose even light. Skip sunglasses and strong face filters.
  • Keep hair and facial features visible in both photos.
  • Use photos you have permission to use and share.
  • For a finished clip, check faces, hands and the leap-to-hug transition.

BEFORE YOU START

Zombie Love Story AI questions

What is the Zombie Love Story AI trend?

It is a two-person zombie story: one person turns, the other cannot shoot, and a leap in a cold cabin becomes a hug in a warm field memory.

How do I prepare my Zombie Love Story AI video?

Choose two clear photos and assign the survivor to the first slot and their partner to the second. Preview them here, then open Pricing to check plans and story support before checkout.

Can either person play the zombie?

Yes. The roles do not depend on gender. Choose two adults who agreed to appear, and decide who stays human and who turns.

Is the field scene a cure?

The field represents a memory before the infection. The transition follows the leap into a past embrace; the partner is not cured inside the cabin.

Can I prepare the story with one photo?

The story has two distinct characters. Use one clear photo for each person so you can plan both roles.

Are my photos uploaded or saved for checkout?

No. The photo slots use local browser previews. Your images are not uploaded, saved to an account or transferred to Pricing.

Does the reference clip show what this site will generate?

No. The embedded clip comes from AI Zombie Video and shows the story idea. It is not a Burning Bridges output or a promise of matching faces, motion, duration or sound.
