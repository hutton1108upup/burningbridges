Frequently asked questions

What is Outstanding Tiny Desk AI?

It is a band-performance AI parody with a desk-side concert setting, a lead close-up and instrument roles associated with the Outstanding reference.

Can I use a group photo?

Yes. Choose One group photo and select a clear image of up to five adults. Check every face and plan the role order before continuing.

Do I need five individual photos?

No. In A photo each mode, the lead portrait is required and the keyboard, saxophone and two backing-singer photos are optional.

Where does Generate video take me?

The Hero button takes you to the photo workspace. After a valid group image or lead portrait is selected in the active mode, the workspace button opens the pricing page.

What photo types can I choose?

Choose JPG, PNG or WebP images up to 10 MB. Use clear, permitted portraits with visible faces and enough of the upper body to understand the clothing.

Can I make an Outstanding Tiny Desk AI video for free?

You can watch the references and prepare the photos without buying credits. Review current generation and download terms for any stated trial or free allowance.

Is this an official NPR performance?

No. This is an independent AI-parody workflow, not an official NPR performance.
