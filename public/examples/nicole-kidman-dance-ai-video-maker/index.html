Keep both dancers visible

Allow space above the heads and beside the bodies. A portrait crop that cuts off hands or shoes makes movement harder to assess.

Break the routine into beats

Watch the overhead gestures, head-level hand pose, wide arm shape and turn. Test a short section before trying to connect every action.

Check faces through the turn

Pause when a face turns away or a hand crosses it. Compare each dancer with their own starting photo, then check the feet and background.
