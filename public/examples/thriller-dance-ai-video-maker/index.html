What is a Thriller dance AI video?

It is a photo-led zombie dance concept with one main dancer and a group behind them. The references show a misty night street and a short coordinated routine.

What photo should I choose?

Start with one person, visible eyes and a clear outfit. For a custom dance scene, leave room for the arms and legs. This local preview accepts JPG, PNG and WebP up to 10 MB.
