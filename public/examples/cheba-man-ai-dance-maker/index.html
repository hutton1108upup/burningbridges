What is the AI Cheba Man dance?

The Cheba Man dance reference pairs a vivid red stage with a night-party setting and a tall portrait frame. The AI example changes the performer while following the dance in the original clip.

The reference site describes two approaches: face swap, which aims to keep the original outfit and scene, and character & outfit, which takes the person and clothes from a full-body photo. The example here is labelled as a face swap; it does not demonstrate both modes.

A few good questions

Cheba Man AI dance maker FAQ.

What is a Cheba Man AI dance maker?

It is a photo-to-video tool for putting a person into the Cheba Man red-stage dance. This page compares the original clip with a AI face-swap example and lets you preview a portrait locally.

How do face swap and character & outfit differ?

The reference site describes face swap as replacing the face and visible hair while aiming to keep the original outfit, body and scene. Character & outfit uses the person and clothes from a full-body photo. These modes are described for context and are not controls on this page.

What photo should I choose?

Use a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB for this local preview. Choose one well-lit person with their face and shoulders visible, and use only photos you have permission to use.

Can I hear the reference music?

Yes. The references start muted. Use the video controls to turn on sound; the soundtrack belongs to the reference clip.

Is this an official ChebaMan Dance page?

No. This is an independent Burning Bridges AI Video reference page. The original and AI clips are sourced from ChebaMan Dance and are not Burning Bridges customer results.
