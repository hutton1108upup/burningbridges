BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers

About the references, your portrait and the available plans.

What is the Toc Toc dance?

The reference clips show a living-room dance built around a knee lift, arms held out and a bounce on the beat. Watch both examples to compare the movement and framing.

What can I do on this page?

Watch the two dance references and preview a portrait on your device. Selecting a photo does not create or submit a video generation task.

Which photo should I choose?

Choose one clear, well-lit photo with a visible face. A full-body photo is useful when planning the outfit and shoes. JPG, PNG and WebP files up to 8 MB are supported.

Are my photos uploaded or saved?

No. The photo preview stays in this browser tab. Removing the photo, reloading the page or closing the tab clears it.

Will buying credits generate a Toc Toc video?

No. Current credit plans cover the Burning Bridges party template. Buying a plan does not select or generate the Toc Toc scene.

Do the example videos have sound?

Use the video player controls to turn sound on. Both reference clips start muted. For your own edit, use audio you have permission to use.
