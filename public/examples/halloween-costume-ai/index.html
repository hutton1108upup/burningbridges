What is Halloween Costume AI?

It is a costume-reveal video concept: an everyday outfit changes into a witch look through a snap, a spin and a burst of glowing effects. Watch the third-party clips here to explore the transition.

What photo should I prepare?

Choose one person facing the camera with a clearly visible face and even lighting. A full-body image helps you plan the before-and-after outfit. Use a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB.
