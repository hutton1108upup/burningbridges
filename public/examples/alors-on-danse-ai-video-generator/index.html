What is Alors on Danse AI?

A familiar performance becomes a new pairing. The Alors on Danse AI trend places two recognizable faces into a stripped-back white studio, assigning one to the keyboard and the other to the laptop.

The fun is in the contrast: friends, unlikely characters or two different looks of the same person. Keep the roles distinct and let the simple setting carry the scene.

Common questions

Alors on Danse AI
Video Generator FAQ.

What is the Alors on Danse AI Video Generator trend?

It recreates the look of a two-person performance in a white studio, with one person at a keyboard and a partner beside a laptop. The examples show different pairings in that familiar scene.

Do the two photos leave my device?

No. Both portrait previews run in your browser. Selecting, replacing or removing a photo here does not upload it to a server.

Which photos should I choose?

Use a separate JPG, PNG or WebP for each role, up to 10 MB per image. Choose sharp, evenly lit portraits with one visible face. Include shoulders when you want to compare outfits.

Can I use two photos of myself?

Yes. You can preview two different looks of yourself, or pair your photo with a friend who has agreed to take part. Each role has its own photo picker.

Do I need a prompt for the photo preview?

No prompt is needed to preview your portraits. Choose the keyboard player and laptop partner separately, then compare your pairing with the reference videos.

Does the page provide the original song or an official artist product?

No. Burning Bridges AI Video is independent of Stromae and Jamel Debbouze. Use music and portraits you have permission to publish.
