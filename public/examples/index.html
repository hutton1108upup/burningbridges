Watch the existing references

Know what you are using: These clips are reference samples for the prepared party scene, not a result generated from your photo. Use the site's four-step flow to upload, sign in, generate and review your own result; identity and motion can vary.

These files were retained with the site's public-page replica. They are presented for inspecting creative direction; their originating model, source portrait and generation settings are not documented here. No before-and-after comparison or customer-result claim is made.

What to inspect

Part of the clip Question to ask
Opening Can you identify one clear lead and the frame shape?
Gesture Do hands and props stay plausible during the movement?
Turn or overlap Does the face change when the head turns or a hand passes?
Background Do the guests stay distinct from the lead?
Audio Is a track present, and does its timing match the action?

Plan your own version

Use the references to decide what you want to change. Prepare a clear portrait, compare creation methods and adapt an original prompt. Access to a reference file does not grant permission to reuse its footage or soundtrack in another tool or post.
