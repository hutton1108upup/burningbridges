Frequently asked questions

What is a Bby Wow Guy AI video?

It is a photo-based remix of the party performance shown in the reference clips. A different lead appearance is combined with a related sequence of poses and gestures.

Which photo should I choose?

Choose one sharp JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB. Keep the face, hair and upper body visible, with room around the arms. Use a photo you have permission to animate.

How do I start making my video?

Compare the reference clips, choose your portrait and select Generate video to review pricing and credit plans. Check the creation details before confirming a generation.

Is this a face swap or a photo-to-video remix?

A face swap replaces a face in existing footage. A photo-to-video remix can reinterpret hair, clothing and the body as well. Compare the whole performer, not just the face.
