Common questions

AI Smalltown Boy FAQ.

What is AI Smalltown Boy?

It is a two-character dance effect inspired by the Smalltown Boy song. Reference clips show expressive reactions and changing positions between two performers.

How many photos should I prepare?

Prepare two separate portraits, one per character. Both photo selectors work independently. Keep each face and the shoulders clearly visible.

Which photo formats can I use?

Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB per image. Use sharp, well-lit portraits and only use photos you have permission to use.

Can I hear the music?

Yes. Use the player controls to enable sound. Reference audio is part of the third-party clips and does not promise a soundtrack or music license in our generator.

Can I use a pet or character?

You can preview a pet or character image in either slot. Local previews do not confirm that a generator supports the subject or can reproduce this dance.
