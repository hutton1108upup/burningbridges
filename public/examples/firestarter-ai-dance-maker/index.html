Firestarter AI dance maker FAQ.

How do I turn my photo into a Firestarter dance video?

Choose one clear portrait, compare the Firestarter references and review the creation settings and credit details. After generation, inspect the face, hands and full crop before downloading and sharing.

Can I use a selfie or a full-body photo?

You can select one JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB in the photo area. Use a wider portrait when the dance shows arms or legs; a tight selfie has less detail about the outfit and body.

Does choosing my photo upload it?

Your selected photo stays in your browser. You can replace or remove it at any time.

How do I prepare my Firestarter photo on a phone?

Choose a sharp portrait from your photo library and check the face, shoulders and arms in the preview. A waist-up or full-body photo is a useful starting point for the dance.

What should I check in a free Firestarter trial?

Check whether the allowance covers the dance mode you want, how many attempts it includes, and whether you can download the result. Video length, resolution and watermark rules can differ between offers.

How do I add the Firestarter song?

Add audio you have permission to use in your video editor or through the posting platform’s music library. Align the strongest arm gesture with the beat and listen to the exported clip before posting.
