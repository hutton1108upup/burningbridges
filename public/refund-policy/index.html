Refund Policy

Refund policy for Burning Bridges AI Video credit purchases

Overview

This policy covers credit purchases, subscriptions, failed video tasks and billing issues. Payment refunds and the return of generation credits are different actions.

One-Time Packs

Packs are $5.90 for 100 credits, $13.90 for 200 and $29.90 for 500. The 100- and 200-credit packs are valid for 30 days; the 500-credit pack for 90 days from credit issuance. Used credits are generally not refundable solely for a change of creative preference, subject to applicable rights and service defects.

Unused Credits and Monthly Plans

Unused credits follow the validity and billing terms shown for the purchase. Credits are not transferable or exchangeable for cash outside an approved refund. Starter, Creator and Studio cost $19, $39 and $79 monthly and include 400, 900 and 2,000 credits per billing month.

Canceling a subscription stops future renewals. It does not itself refund completed periods or create a cash refund for unused credits. Keep your order reference for billing support.

Failed-Task Credits

If a charged task is confirmed to have failed without delivering a result, its generation credits should be returned to the relevant balance. This is a credit adjustment, not a refund to a payment card. Contact support with task and order references if the adjustment is missing.

Payment Refund Requests

Contact support about duplicate payments, incorrect charges or service failures that prevented delivery. Include the receipt, relevant account email and a description. Submit a billing request within 7 days where possible; this support timeframe does not limit applicable legal rights.

Payment refunds are considered against the order, credit use and reason for the request. Processing time depends on the provider and payment method; no fixed settlement time is promised.

Consumer Rights

Applicable consumer rights remain in effect. Starting to use credits does not by itself establish that every withdrawal right has been waived. Any exception for immediately supplied digital content or services depends on applicable requirements, including notices, consent and confirmation.

Creative Results and Misuse

A creative preference is different from a confirmed technical failure or billing error. Requests involving misuse, unauthorized payments or disputed ownership require review; they do not automatically remove non-excludable rights.

Billing Disputes

Contact support first if possible so we can review the order. Do not send card numbers or security codes. You may use available payment-provider dispute channels; a dispute is not an automatic forfeiture of all rights.

Changes

We update this policy as needed. Terms shown for an order and applicable rights remain relevant to that purchase.

Contact

A confirmed cash refund removes the corresponding unused credits, proportionately for a partial refund. Failed video tasks return their deducted credits once failure is confirmed.
