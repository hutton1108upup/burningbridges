Who originally released Smalltown Boy?

Smalltown Boy is Bronski Beat’s 1984 debut single, with Jimmy Somerville singing. London Records identifies the original trio as Somerville, Steve Bronski and Larry Steinbachek. Official Charts records a first UK chart entry on June 2, 1984 and a peak of number three. Sources: London Records’ artist history; Official Charts, Smalltown Boy.

The record existed decades before photo-animation tools. A template named after it is not the source of the music.

Why does the song’s background matter?

London Records describes the group’s gay activism and the song’s lasting importance for LGBTQI+ and other marginalized listeners. Reducing it to a funny pairing clip leaves out that context.

There is more than one dance revival

Know Your Meme documents a separate “Dance Like It’s the 80s” format in spring 2024: people asked parents to dance as they had in the 1980s, commonly using Smalltown Boy. This documents a social-video revival, not the song’s origin. Source: Know Your Meme, Dance Like It’s the 80s.
