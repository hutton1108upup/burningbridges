How to Make an Alors on Danse AI Video

Prepare an Alors on Danse keyboard-and-laptop duet. Assign the two portraits, plan the white-studio framing, and check hands, props and timing in a draft.

1. Choose the result and workflow

Start here: Look for explicit two-image or role-based input support. Compose the studio and both characters first, then test a short interaction. If a tool accepts only one identity image, do not assume that putting two portraits side by side will reliably assign them to different roles.

2. Prepare the photo inputs

Prepare one portrait per role. Keep both faces unobstructed and use similar levels of detail. A very sharp close-up paired with a tiny cropped face makes a poor comparison. Decide who takes the keyboard before choosing the laptop-side portrait.

Open the local photo preparation area to inspect the selected files. Follow the format and size rules printed beside its inputs. Then check the requirements of your chosen creation tool separately; they may differ.

3. Plan a short sequence

Establish the workspace

Show the keyboard and laptop in a clear studio layout. Leave space between the two people so each role can be read.

Keep the keyboard role stable

The player should stay oriented toward the keys while still showing enough face. Hands need to remain attached and clear of the other person.

Give the partner a reaction

Use a small glance, nod or expression from the laptop side. A brief reaction can carry the joke without a complicated camera move.

Use these as separate composition checks. A service with multi-shot controls may accept them together; otherwise, test a short shot first and join usable clips in an editor. Do not assume a single upload supports every camera change.

4. Adapt an original starting prompt

For a compatible image-to-video tool, this is a starting idea rather than a tested preset or a guarantee of the reference choreography. Keep only the actions your selected model can handle.

Two adults in a bright white studio, one on the left at an electronic keyboard and one on the right beside a laptop. The keyboard player makes a small hand movement while the partner gives a restrained nod. Keep both identities and their positions distinct, preserve the equipment shapes, stable medium-wide framing, no text.

Choose the appearance references through the tool’s supported input controls. A written instruction to preserve a face cannot replace a missing identity input. Adjust the framing to your intended output before spending credits on longer clips.

5. Review the first draft

The performers swap places
Write down a left-right role map and preserve it in the starting image. Avoid cutting to a reversed angle during the first test.
Hands melt into the keys
Reduce finger motion and use a wider view. The interaction can read through posture without showing every finger pressing a different key.
The laptop blocks a face
Lower or offset the equipment in the composition before animation. Do not rely on a crop to rescue a hidden face later.

6. Edit, check sound and export

Trim the start and ending so the sequence has a clear setup and a settled finish. Avoid using an elaborate transition to hide a face or hand error: compare a simpler shot first. If several clips are joined, check screen direction and identity on both sides of every cut.

Use audio you are entitled to use and inspect the actual export. Check whether the tool includes audio, allows downloads and adds a watermark before rendering. The Alors on Danse free-method guide gives an editing alternative when you prefer to avoid paid AI tests.

Use real filming or split-screen editing for control over reactions. Use a two-role generation workflow for a new synthetic pairing, after checking identity separation and equipment handling. The local planner is useful for comparing portraits, not testing those model capabilities.

Frequently asked questions

Which photo belongs to which Alors on Danse role?

The local planner labels the left role as the keyboard player and the right role as the laptop partner. Each portrait can be replaced independently.

Does the reference audio come with every result?

No such promise is made here. Check the selected tool’s audio and export terms, and use audio permitted for your intended post.
