How to Make an Alors on Danse AI Duet Video

To make an Alors on Danse AI video, prepare two separate portraits and assign one to the keyboard player and the other to the laptop partner. Choose a two-person studio template, check the framing and sound options, then review both performers through the close-ups. Role order matters more than a long prompt.

Keep the keyboard player and partner distinct

The version covered here is the white-studio duet with a keyboard and laptop. One performer plays while the partner reacts. Start by watching the references on our project page; a generic dance effect bearing the song title may not recreate this particular setup.

Write the roles next to the photo filenames before uploading. Use labels such as keyboard and partner rather than relying only on left and right, because a close-up or camera cut can change screen position. The same person can supply two different looks if that is the pairing you want.

Prepare two portraits with similar clarity

Choose one person per photo with the whole face visible. Include shoulders and some clothing so each role has a readable appearance. The photos do not need matching backgrounds, but neither face should be much smaller or blurrier than the other.

Our local duet workspace accepts JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB per portrait. Its left slot is the keyboard player and its right slot is the laptop partner. You can replace either image independently. The pairing preview compares your selections; it does not show how a generator will animate them.

Before uploading elsewhere, check the provider's role map again. Keep your original files available: images selected in our preview do not move to the external site or to Burning Bridges checkout.

For more framing checks, see the photo preparation guide; use this project’s stated file limits where they differ.

Compare the whole duet, including sound

The Alorsondanse.video provider describes separate keyboard and instructor inputs and a silent download. This is a useful reminder to check audio explicitly: a reference playing a song does not establish that the finished file includes it. Other providers may make different choices.

Start with a landscape composition when keeping both performers and their equipment visible is your priority. For a vertical post, inspect the whole action before cropping. A centered crop can preserve one face while cutting off the partner's gesture or the keyboard. Consider a padded layout when the wide scene needs to stay intact.

Make and review your first video

  1. Choose the studio scene

    Open a provider whose sample matches the keyboard-and-partner duet. Watch past the opening frame to confirm that the reactions and close-ups are included. Do not confuse this scene with a solo dance to the same song.

  2. Assign each photo by role

    Place the keyboard portrait and partner portrait in the matching fields. If a swap control is available, verify both previews after using it. A pair of correct photos in the wrong slots still produces the wrong casting.

  3. Check frame, duration and sound

    Read the selected output settings and price before submitting. Confirm whether the file is silent or includes audio. The dimensions of an embedded sample are not a substitute for the actual output selection.

  4. Render and compare both performers

    Review the face at the keyboard, the reacting partner, and any close-up where a person approaches the lens. Check hands near keys and the laptop as well as expressions. A stable background alone does not show that the duet worked.

  5. Prepare the final post

    If the file is silent, choose permitted audio in your editor or publishing app. Align the reaction to the music, then watch the complete export. Keep both roles visible and identify the clip as AI-created.

Fix the most visible problem first

The two identities swap
Recheck the role inputs. If they were correct, save the problem frame and retry with clearer separate portraits. A group photo can make the role assignment harder to interpret.
The keyboard disappears in a vertical crop
Return to the wider render and reframe it. Keep the instruments and relevant hand movements visible, or use a layout that preserves the full landscape image.
The downloaded video has no song
Check whether the provider intentionally exports a silent file. Add audio separately only after reviewing the permitted uses for your intended post.

Save the first draft and change one input at a time. The video troubleshooting guide explains how to separate input problems from a completed result that needs revision.

Frequently asked questions

Do the two people need to be photographed together?

No. The workflow described here uses a separate portrait for each role. Our local page also previews them independently.

Can one person play both roles?

You can plan the duet with two photos of yourself. Check whether the chosen generator accepts that casting and inspect the resulting role consistency.

Can Burning Bridges generate this duet?

No. The duet page offers references and two local photo previews. Its Pricing links lead to the separate Burning Bridges party template.
