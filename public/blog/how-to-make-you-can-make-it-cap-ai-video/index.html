How to Make a You Can Make It Cap AI Video from a Photo

To make a You Can Make It Cap AI video, start with one clear portrait and the studio-reaction reference. This guide covers photo selection, the maker and the checks to make before sharing.

By Burning Bridges AI Video · Updated October 27, 2026

In this guide
  • 1. Choose a clear portrait
  • 2. Watch the reaction
  • 3. Add your photo to the maker
  • 4. Review video plans
  • 5. Check your finished edit
  • Frequently asked questions

1. Choose a clear portrait

Choose one person facing the camera with even light, an unobstructed face and enough of the upper body to show clothing. Use a photo you have permission to animate. Avoid sunglasses, extreme filters and group shots.

2. Watch the reaction

Play the reference and note the change in expression, the hand near the mouth and the turn of the head. A similar trend name can describe a different scene, so check the video itself before continuing.

3. Add your photo to the maker

Open the maker and choose a JPG, PNG or WebP file up to 10 MB. Review the image in the photo workspace. You can replace or remove it before continuing.

4. Review video plans

Select Generate video in the photo workspace after choosing a valid image. The button opens pricing. Review the displayed plan and creation details before deciding to continue.

5. Check your finished edit

When reviewing a result, look at the face during the laugh and the hand near the mouth. Check the framing and sound through the entire clip, and follow the sharing platform’s rules for AI content and audio.

Frequently asked questions

How many photos do I need?

The maker asks for one clear portrait.

Can I use a selfie?

A clear selfie can work as a starting photo; an upper-body image provides more detail about the outfit and shoulders.

Does choosing a photo start generation?

Choosing a photo displays its preview in the workspace. Select Generate video to review pricing.
