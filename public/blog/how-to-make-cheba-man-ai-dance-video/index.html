How to Make a Cheba Man AI Dance Video

To make a Cheba Man AI dance video, first decide whether to keep the dancer's outfit or replace the whole character. Prepare a portrait for a face swap, or a fuller body photo when clothing matters, then use a compatible red-stage dance effect. Check the face during turns and the clothing during movement before sharing.

By Burning Bridges AI Video · October 20, 2026

Choose between a face swap and a new outfit

The red-stage reference is useful because it gives you a specific performance to compare. Watch the original and the AI face-swap example on our project page. The example demonstrates one interpretation of the dance; it does not establish that every available mode produces the same result.

The maker of ChebaMan Dance describes two modes: Face swap keeps the template body and outfit while using the supplied face; Character & outfit uses the reference person and clothing. Those descriptions are provider claims. Choose based on what you need to change, and inspect the selected mode's own preview before paying.

Prepare the photo for your selected mode

For a face swap, use one well-lit face with the hairline visible and enough space around the head. Avoid strong beauty filters that remove distinguishing details. A side-profile source is harder to compare with a mostly frontal performance, so pick a viewing angle close to the reference when possible.

For a clothing change, include the outfit down to the shoes. Leave space around the arms and legs instead of cropping tightly. If your only image is a headshot, it does not show the trousers or shoes you want preserved. Find a fuller photo rather than expecting the tool to infer those details.

Our Cheba Man page accepts JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB for local portrait preview. Choosing a photo here does not upload it to ChebaMan Dance or start a render. The external provider may apply different file checks.

For more framing checks, see the photo preparation guide; use this project’s stated file limits where they differ.

Use the actual red-stage effect

Open the provider's effect page and compare its scene with the red-stage reference. If it has separate face and character modes, choose the intended one before adding the image. Read the preview, cost and output terms at that point; settings associated with another mode may not carry over.

A text-to-video dance idea is an alternative if you want a new scene, not the same choreography. Keep any original prompt focused on one performer, visible footwork and a stable stage. Exact body positions and timing require more direction than the name of a dance.

Workflow source: ChebaMan Dance provider page. Provider descriptions checked October 20, 2026; no paid render was tested for this guide.

Make and review your first video

  1. Watch a full movement cycle

    Choose a head turn, an arm gesture and a step to compare later. Use the original and AI reference side by side to see which visual details matter most to you.

  2. Choose and upload the appropriate photo

    Use a portrait for face-led replacement or a full-body image for a mode that takes clothing from the source. Check that the uploaded preview still includes the parts you need.

  3. Confirm the selected mode and cost

    Read the current generation controls and download conditions. Our Pricing page is for the Burning Bridges party effect; it does not purchase a Cheba Man render from another site.

  4. Generate one draft and inspect it

    Follow the provider's task status. When a result is ready, inspect the face during turns, clothing edges near the arms, and both shoes against the floor. Save the draft before deciding whether to retry.

Fix the most visible problem first

The face is right but the clothes are unchanged
You may have selected a face-only mode. Check the mode description before changing the photo. A face swap is not a promise to replace the full outfit.
Clothing changes halfway through
Compare the source image's visible outfit with the affected frame. Use a less obstructed full-body image for a character mode and keep the next attempt otherwise similar.
The face flickers under stage lighting
Watch the entire turn at normal speed and frame by frame. Try an evenly lit input rather than a photo with a strong colored shadow across the face.

Save the first draft and change one input at a time. The video troubleshooting guide explains how to separate input problems from a completed result that needs revision.

Frequently asked questions

Which photo should I use for Cheba Man AI?

Use a clear portrait for a face swap. If the selected mode also takes clothing from your image, use a full-body photo with the outfit and shoes visible.

Is the reference audio included in every result?

Do not assume so. Check the selected provider's current output description and listen to the saved file before adding music.

Can I generate Cheba Man on this site?

No. Our page shows credited comparison clips and a local photo preview. Buying Burning Bridges credits does not unlock a Cheba Man template.

Put the plan into practice

Start with the Cheba Man AI Dance Maker reference, choose the inputs described above, and check one complete draft before making more versions.
