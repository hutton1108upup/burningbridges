What can I do before buying credits?

Watch the examples and choose a portrait in the maker. You can replace or remove the photo before looking at the plans.

Can I film an angel reveal myself?

Yes. If you have a costume and a place to film, record a door opening or a reveal in a room. Leave enough space for the wings and check the background before filming.

Does a free preview include a video?

Not necessarily. A photo preview does not include a finished video. Check the current credit and download terms before you start.

Where can I see the current plans?

Choose a photo in the maker, then select Generate video to open Pricing. Compare the plans and their terms before continuing.

Can I use the song in my post?

Listen to your finished video before posting. If it includes music, check whether you can use that track on your chosen platform.

Frequently asked questions

Can I select a photo without paying?

Yes. You can select, replace or remove a photo before reviewing the plans.

Does a free photo preview guarantee a free video?

No. Check the current video generation and download conditions.

Can I film a version without AI?

Yes. You can film your own costume reveal in a suitable location.

Can I reuse the reference music?

A reference does not automatically grant music rights. Check the terms for your intended use.
