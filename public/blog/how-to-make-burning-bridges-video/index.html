How to Make a Burning Bridges AI Video: A Step-by-Step Walkthrough

A practical Burning Bridges AI video tutorial: choosing the photo, picking a ratio and resolution, reading the credit cost, and reviewing the finished party clip.

Oct 8, 2026 Burning Bridges AI Video

Prepare Your Burning Bridges Video

Choose a portrait, understand the reference, compare credit options and review a result. Check your settings before submitting any paid task.

Before You Start

  • One image under 8MB, showing one clear person or character.
  • Even lighting and an unobstructed face.
  • The upper body if clothing matters.
  • Permission to use the portrait and media you publish.

Step 1: Choose Your Photo

Select a portrait and inspect the local image preview. The counter shows one attached reference. Avoid blur, heavy filters and multiple faces.

Step 2: Compare Settings and Credits

16:9 matches the landscape reference; 1:1 is square and 9:16 is vertical. Check composition before choosing a narrow frame. Estimates are 50 credits for 480P, 100 for 720P Standard and 200 for 1080P Pro. More pixels do not guarantee a closer likeness.

Step 3: Choose a Plan and Review the Job

Continue to pricing for monthly plans and one-time packs. Photo selection and example playback do not consume generation credits. Payment, credit availability and task submission are separate steps: successful payment is not itself a completed video.

Task Status and Billing Issues

Check the status associated with a submitted task. If a charged task is confirmed to have failed without delivering a result, its generation credits should be returned under the refund policy. Keep job and order references for support.

Check Picture and Sound

Watch for identity changes, distorted hands, overlaps and clothing details. Listen for missing audio or timing drift. Examples show creative directions, not an exact outcome for a different portrait.

Browsing is free. Credit packs start at $9.90 and monthly plans at $19. Review validity, billing frequency and job estimates before purchasing.
