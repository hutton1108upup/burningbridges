Before you make your party-video version

A Drake Burning Bridges AI video is a party-scene version with your portrait guiding the lead. This site is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Drake. Follow the site flow below; external methods are optional.

Know what you are using: Upload one permitted photo, sign in and use credits when needed, then generate with the site's fixed party template. The reference video guides the prepared motion and scene; check the completed picture and sound before downloading. Results can vary and a reference clip is not a promise of identical identity or motion.

Bring one permitted portrait under 8MB and confirm you have permission to use it. The site uses a fixed party template for clothing, scene and movement; the method comparison is optional background reading.

Make your video on this site

  1. Open the photo form, upload one permitted photo under 8MB and check the visible preview.
  2. Sign in and buy credits if your balance is insufficient. Existing credits can be used directly.
  3. Return to the photo form. The template uses fixed 720P, 16:9 output and 100 credits per generation. Click Generate Video and follow the task status while the fixed template is rendered.
  4. When the task is complete, review the picture and audio, then download your video.
Your photo selection and unfinished task can be restored in this browser. Removing the photo or signing out clears the draft; drafts older than seven days are cleared on a later visit. The site requires permission to use the uploaded photo and may send it to the configured generation provider when you submit.

Optional alternative workflows

The site flow above does not require an external tool or a written prompt. These alternatives are optional references for people exploring a different creative direction; use each service's official interface and check its current limits and price before submitting.

  1. For image to video, attach the portrait, use an original party prompt and choose supported settings.
  2. For motion transfer, bring a performance clip you own or have permission to transform, plus the appearance image. Check the tool's source-video requirements.
  3. For CapCut editing, choose a relevant current template and inspect what media it requires. An editing template can arrange footage without generating a new face performance.
  4. Generate or export, watch the full result, then add permitted audio in the publishing platform or editor.

See Higgsfield’s official workflow and Dreamina’s party-scene guide. These are external services with their own accounts and costs.

Check the result, not just the first frame

  • Watch the face during turns and occlusion.
  • Check both hands and the cup for shape changes.
  • Review background faces, clothing and unwanted text.
  • Check composition in the actual output dimensions.
  • Listen for missing, duplicated or misaligned audio.
