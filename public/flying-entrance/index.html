Common questions

Flying Entrance FAQ

What is the Flying Entrance trend?

Flying Entrance is an AI Car Jump Video idea: a person sits facing the camera while a car jumps over a bump and lands behind the chair. The calm pose and dramatic background motion create the contrast.

What happens in the Flying Entrance video?

The scene starts in a golden field with a person seated in a black camp chair. A car rises into the air behind them, passes through a slow-motion arc and lands in a cloud of dust. A finger snap or a dirt shower can finish the scene.

How do I prepare an AI Car Jump Video with my own photos?

Choose a clear photo of yourself and a front-facing photo of your car. Preview both on this page, then check the supported templates on Pricing. Credit plans cover the Burning Bridges party template.

How much does Flying Entrance cost?

Watching the reference and previewing your photos here are free. Our paid credit plans cover the Burning Bridges party template, rather than the Flying Entrance effect. See Pricing for current packs and subscriptions.

What photos should I use?

Use one well-lit photo of yourself, preferably with your full body visible, and one clear photo of the car from the front. Avoid crowded backgrounds, heavy filters and objects covering your face. Only use photos you have permission to use.

Will my result match the example exactly?

AI results can vary in faces, car details, motion and lighting. The reference shows the scene idea, rather than a guarantee of identical output. Check the finished video before sharing it.

How long is the reference video?

The reference is about 12 seconds long and uses a vertical 9:16 frame. Check the selected plan and template for the available output length, resolution and audio settings.

Can I share an AI Car Jump Video on social media?

A vertical 9:16 video suits TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Review the final file and follow the platform’s rules for labeling AI-generated content.

Do I need to install an app?

You can browse the reference and compare plans in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. This page does not require an app installation.
