Flying Entrance questions

What is the Flying Entrance trend?

Flying Entrance is an AI Car Jump Video idea: a person sits facing the camera while a car jumps over a bump and lands behind the chair. The calm pose and dramatic background motion create the contrast.

What happens in the Flying Entrance video?

The scene starts in a golden field with a person seated in a black camp chair. A car rises into the air behind them, passes through a slow-motion arc and lands in a cloud of dust. A finger snap or a dirt shower can finish the scene.

What photos should I use?

Use one well-lit photo of yourself, preferably with your full body visible, and one clear photo of the car from the front. Avoid crowded backgrounds, heavy filters and objects covering your face. Only use photos you have permission to use.

Will my result match the example exactly?

AI results can vary in faces, car details, motion and lighting. The reference shows the scene idea, rather than a guarantee of identical output. Check the finished video before sharing it.

How long is the reference video?
