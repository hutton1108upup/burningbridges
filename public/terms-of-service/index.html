Prohibited Content

The AIGC rules below apply to photo references, instructions, generated or simulated videos, audio and use of our service. They also apply to attempts to bypass safety controls.

AIGC Content Standards

  • Sexual or NSFW content: pornography, explicit sexual acts, sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery and sexualized nudity are prohibited.
  • Violence or gore: graphic injury, torture, threats, promotion of violence, terrorism or instructions for violent harm are prohibited.
  • Hate speech: dehumanization, discriminatory abuse or incitement against people based on protected characteristics are prohibited.
  • Child-unsafe content: child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexualization of minors, grooming, exploitation or content that endangers children are prohibited. This includes synthetic depictions.
  • Deepfakes or impersonation: deceptive impersonation, fraud, non-consensual use of a real person's likeness and all non-consensual sexual deepfakes are prohibited. Use another person's image only with permission; do not present an AI video as evidence of a real event or endorsement.

We cannot inspect a browser-local photo unless you choose to provide appropriate evidence.

Before enabling real AI generation, we will identify the generation model and the input and output safety tools, and implement checks before submission and before release of a result.
